# 《點石齋畫報》上海同仁醫院個案二則（1884、1895 年，巴伐利亞邦立圖書館） / Dian shi zhai hua bao [Two Cases at Tongren Hospital in Shanghai]: Transcription Dataset

上海虹口同仁醫院的兩則個案刊於《點石齋畫報》。1884 年〈著手成春〉報導一名婦人因大瘤求醫，就診後由女醫施治。1895 年〈頭上生頭〉記家屬抱初生兒前來診視，院內女醫轉報會診，諸醫因嬰兒體弱建議延後手術。兩篇為晚清報刊如何呈現女醫、患者就醫與院內照護決策的史料。

Two illustrated reports in the Dianshizhai Pictorial describe patients at Tongren (St Luke's) Hospital in Shanghai. The 1884 article Zhuo shou cheng chun describes a woman with a large growth and her treatment by a woman physician. The 1895 article Tou shang sheng tou describes a family bringing a newborn to the hospital, where an attending woman physician sought consultation and the doctors recommended postponing surgery. The reports document how the late Qing illustrated press represented women physicians, care seeking and clinical decisions.

## V0.1.0

兩篇完整圖說共36欄，附篇題、圖號、期號、畫款和印記位置及連讀文本。AI 輔助轉錄，待判字已標記。

Two complete illustrated articles in 36 columns with titles, plate and issue numbers, artists’ signatures, seal locations, and continuous reading text. AI-assisted transcription with uncertain readings marked.

## 方法與覆核 / Methods and review

由 AI 依固定館方影像逐欄讀字，保留原字、版面與疑讀。原字按欄定位。正文結束後另標畫款與印記。已定位的小字未能辨認時保留位置。人類全文雙校、專家審定、同儕審查及準確率量測尚未進行。實際模型識別與使用量未知。此資料公開供比對與引用，處理程式及影像另存。

An AI assistant transcribed the fixed source images column by column, preserving source forms, layout and uncertain readings. Columns are addressable in source reading order. Signatures, seals and illegible illustrated lettering are linked to source regions. Human proofreading, expert review, peer review and accuracy measurement are pending. The actual model identifiers and usage are unknown.
